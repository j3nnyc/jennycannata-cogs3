Mac and Cheese

Ingredients

  • 1 cup elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 3 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup bread crumbs
  • 1 pinch paprika

Directions

  1. In a large pot of boiling salted water, cook pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain well.
  2. In a saucepan over medium heat, melt 1/4 cup butter. Stir in flour to create a roux. Cook, stirring constantly, until mixture is golden brown, about 1 minute. Gradually whisk in milk and cook until thickened, about 5 minutes. Stir in cheeses until melted. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  3. Combine pasta and cheese sauce in the pot, stirring to combine.
  4. In a separate small pan, melt 2 tablespoons butter. Add bread crumbs and cook until golden brown, about 2 minutes. Sprinkle bread crumb mixture over macaroni and cheese. Sprinkle with paprika.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy!
